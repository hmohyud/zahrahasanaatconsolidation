The medical programme brings quality, dignified healthcare to underserved families — especially children — in rural and semi-urban India. Health is rarely a standalone need: it is bound up with a family's nutrition, income and access to education. By meeting medical needs with genuine care and continuity, the programme builds trusted relationships with communities and opens a pathway towards wider family support.

## What we do

### Paediatric surgery camps

Specialist camps bring paediatric surgery, ophthalmology, orthopaedics, neurology, neuro-rehabilitation and speech therapy to rural communities in a single setting. Children who need surgery are identified at camp and their treatment is facilitated at partner hospitals, followed by post-operative care.

### Mother and child health camps

Camps with obstetric, gynaecological and paediatric specialists provide consultations and necessary medications, with counsellors following up on each family's treatment plan.

### Outpatient services

A standing outpatient partnership extends access to consultations between camps, giving families a reliable point of care throughout the year.

### Preventive health and telemedicine

Telemedicine consultations and subsidised testing packages reach people who cannot travel for in-person care, supporting prevention and early detection for high-risk groups.

### Referrals and follow-up

A referral network connects families to partner hospitals for secondary and specialist care, with structured follow-up — surgical, rehabilitative and psychosocial — so no one is left to navigate recovery alone.

## Our approach

The programme follows a continuity model: identification at camp, facilitation of treatment, surgery or specialist care at a partner hospital, and structured post-operative follow-up. Hospital partnerships — including White Lotus International Hospital, MGM Hospital and Wockhardt — make specialist paediatric care accessible at a fraction of its usual cost, and often where it is not otherwise available. Every medical touchpoint is paired with counsellor support, connecting healthcare to nutrition, education and livelihood assistance, so that a single consultation can become the start of holistic support for the whole family.
