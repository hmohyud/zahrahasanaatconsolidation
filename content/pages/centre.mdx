Zahra Hasanaat works through a growing network of neighbourhood centres: locally rooted spaces where people can meet immediate needs while building long-term skills and confidence. Each centre is shaped by its own community, offering a mix of tuition, nutrition support, health camps, wellness activities and skills training. More than a venue for activities, the centre is a welcoming public space for learning, care and shared community life.

## What we do

### Learning and tuition

Centres host daily tuition, literacy and reading circles, homework support, English classes, storytelling and computer literacy, giving children a steady and supportive place to learn.

### Nutrition support

Meals, ration support and child nutrition are combined with practical sessions on cooking and healthy living, addressing immediate need while strengthening family well-being.

### Health camps and care

Medical camps, screenings, referrals, teleconsultation, maternal and child health services and counselling support improve early access to care within the community.

### Wellness and movement

Physical activity, art sessions, Hapkido, reflection circles and youth and women's groups help people stay active, reduce isolation and build healthier habits.

### Skills and livelihoods

Stitching, embroidery, craft, financial literacy, digital literacy and earning pathways for women turn local skill and participation into income potential and long-term independence.

### Volunteering and community life

Each centre is run with local instructors, community volunteers and coordinators, with clear pathways for community members to take on responsibility and grow into local leadership.

## Our approach

Each centre takes its shape from its own neighbourhood: some are education-led, others focus on nutrition, wellness or livelihoods, and the strongest grow into multi-service hubs. People can enter through any doorway — a meal, a tuition class, a stitching group — and move naturally between programmes as their needs and interests change. All centres share a common framework across the network, built on local trust, participation and continuity, so that services reinforce one another rather than working in isolation. Over time, the aim is for community members to move from receiving support to contributing and leading.
