Humrahe Khair grows from a longer tradition of Taqreeb. The word *Taqreeb* means "to bring closer", and the concept has been articulated within Zahra Hasanaat as an active effort to understand both differences and commonalities among communities, so that people may unite for peaceful and constructive purposes.

Taqreeb does not depend on theological agreement. Its strength lies precisely in allowing difference to remain real while refusing to let difference prevent relationship. The aim is to understand one another more deeply, respect differences that cannot be reconciled, and find common ground on which relationships can be built.

## From Taqreeb to Humrahe Khair

Syedna Khuzaima Qutbuddin RA envisioned bringing people of all faiths together to work toward common humanitarian goals and to realise religion's potential as a force for good. Under Syedna Taher Fakhruddin TUS, this vision has increasingly moved from academic and interfaith dialogue toward practical, service-centred collaboration.

[Humrahe Khair](hek.html) represents this next step. If Taqreeb asks how people can come closer in understanding, Humrahe Khair asks what becomes possible once that relationship exists. Its answer is shared service.

### The Taqreeb approach

* Create welcoming spaces for dialogue and relationship-building.
* Begin with shared concern for human dignity and well-being.
* Understand both commonalities and differences without requiring homogenisation.
* Move from conversation toward practical opportunities for service.
* Connect religious leaders, practitioners, volunteers, researchers, professionals and institutions.
* Build trust over time through repeated engagement and shared experience.

## What Taqreeb means

Taqreeb is an Arabic word that literally means "to bring closer." It is based on the consonant root letters Q-R-B, with the derivatives *qareeb* (close) and *qurbat* (closeness); many Indian languages, such as Hindi and Urdu, have appropriated this word over the years. In the form *taqreeb*, the word expresses an active effort to "bring closer." As such, it succinctly conveys the mindset of building bridges and bringing people together in peace and harmony.

The concept of *Taqreeb,* as we define it, is the propagation of inter/intra faith and community understanding so that we may better unite for peaceful causes. In our attempt to understand each other's ideologies, it does not mean that we must homogenize diverse groups or ignore differences. It does not mean "taking things lying down" or giving up one's rights or culture. We believe there is nothing passive about *Taqreeb.* It is an active effort to understand our differences as well as commonalities and build bridges. Our hope is that these concepts and ideas will develop into positive reverberations in the communities we live in through grassroots movements and policy decisions.

**2016 · University of Calcutta**

*First Taqreeb Conference: Propagation of Harmonious Relations in Mughal, British and Independent India* — 27–28 September 2016

Organised with the University of Calcutta's Department of Arabic and Persian, the first Taqreeb conference examined the propagation of harmonious relations in India. It brought together scholars and public figures from across the Shia–Sunni spectrum, as well as Hindu and Christian perspectives. The gathering itself became an example of Taqreeb: different narratives meeting in serious conversation about communal harmony and peaceful coexistence.

## Bring Taqreeb to your community

Grassroots bridge-building starts close to home. We hope to feature local initiatives that reach out and build bridges in the communities we live in, and to partner with local organizations that further peaceful relationship building within and between communities. A few ways to begin:

1. Learn about the community you live in and the other communities around you. Start a dialogue and listen to their concerns - it will give you ideas how to get involved.
2. Contact local places of faith, or community centers and introduce yourself. Discuss the concept of "Taqreeb" and figure out ways of starting dialogue between your communities.
3. Contact (email, letter, phone call etc) your representatives and introduce yourself and the initiative of Taqreeb. We are happy to share templates of letters that we have exchanged in the past.

For two examples, read about the [Dubai Iftaar Drive](story-dubai-iftaar-drive.html) and the [Boston Women's Homeless Shelter](story-boston-womens-homeless-shelter.html). If you have started something in your own community, we would love to hear about it.

## Our roots

The Taqreeb Conference Series was initiated by the [Qutbi Jubilee Scholarship Program](qjsp-about.html) (QJSP) - a scholarship, mentoring, and career counseling program dedicated to establishing the importance of higher education in the community, especially with regards to women, which also supports educational projects and conferences that foster harmonious co-existence. The first conference in the series was held in collaboration with the University of Calcutta in September 2016.

![Qutbi Jubilee Scholarship Program logo](/assets/uploads/taqreeb/2017/06/QJSP-logo.png)

The history of the Dawoodi Bohra Community in India and their interactions with other communities are noteworthy for the goal of *taqreeb*. On the one hand, their perpetual status as a minority necessitated some form of pragmatic philosophy of coexistence; on the other hand, their leaders at several points in time, initiated influential movements of *taqreeb*, whose effects were felt nationwide. These movements frequently drew upon the philosophy and history of the Fatimid Caliph-Imams – to whom the Dawoodi Bohras trace their heritage – to articulate a universal and divine origin to all sects and beliefs. In the modern period, a noteworthy example is that of the community's 51st leader, Syedna Taher Saifuddin (1888-1965), who led initiatives to nurture *taqreeb* among religious communities. Through more than 40 treatises and thousands of lines of Arabic devotional poetry, he presented a model of spiritual Islam that seeks to shun all forms and facets of disharmony and enmity among members of humanity over the globe.
