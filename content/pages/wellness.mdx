Good health is built through habits, relationships and daily practice, not one-off interventions. Our Wellness vertical brings together preventive health education, physical activity, nutrition and creative reflection in one coordinated platform, with AMAN as its flagship programme. Each stream offers a different way in — structured coaching, art, movement or community gathering — towards the same aims: healthier lives, stronger relationships and less isolation.

## What we do

### AMAN wellness cohorts

Small-group, six-month programmes combining weekly live online education sessions, twice-weekly exercise sessions with yoga, stretching and functional training, an individualised nutrition consultation in the first six weeks, and optional accountability challenges that support gradual, sustainable change.

### Art circles

Weekly two-hour small-group sessions built around check-in and grounding, art-making, sharing and reflection. The approach is process-oriented rather than product-oriented, offering a safe, non-judgemental space for self-awareness, healing and connection.

### Hapkido self-defence classes

Twice-weekly martial arts classes that develop confidence, fitness, discipline, stamina, flexibility and emotional regulation, with clear progression and belt examinations every three months. Classes are designed especially for women, girls and families, and fee waivers ensure no one is excluded for financial reasons.

### ArtWell Collective

A grassroots community art space offering wellness workshops, open studio access, creative counselling support, social spotlight exhibitions, and dialogue and reflection circles. Pilot activities include a drum circle, mind mapping and a craft-art lab, encouraging slow attention, tactile creation and face-to-face connection.

## Our approach

The vertical is designed as one platform with four interrelated streams, because people come to wellness through different doors — some through health education and coaching, others through art, self-defence or small-group gathering. AMAN's four-part framework organises the whole: Awareness, Mindfulness, Activity and Nutrition. The programmes follow a social business model — fair fees for those who can pay, structured fee waivers for those who cannot, and fair value for facilitators — so that wellness remains both accessible and sustainable.
