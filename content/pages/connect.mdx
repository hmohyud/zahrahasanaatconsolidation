Zahra Hasanaat is built by the people who take part in it. Our aspiration is that everyone — not just a handful — actively participates in building the community with their expertise, suggestions, knowledge and skills.

Zahra Hasanaat welcomes new ideas and volunteers. You can connect with us if you are looking to apply and receive assistance, or if you'd like to offer your skills, ideas and energy, or just simply say hello.
