Humrahe Khair 2025 brought faith leaders, nonprofits, philanthropists, academics and grassroots activists into one room to work on a shared question: how do we tackle poverty and its root causes together, rather than separately? It was convened by Zahra Hasanaat in Thane on 14 December 2025, and built around conversation between sectors that rarely share a table.

## What came out of it

<CoverCard>
### From conversation to cooperation

The convening was designed to end in commitments rather than applause: organisations mapped what each already does, identified where efforts overlap or leave gaps, and agreed follow-up between sectors that had not previously worked together.
</CoverCard>

Humrahe Khair continues as a standing programme of dialogue gatherings, collaborative service projects and shared resource mapping.
