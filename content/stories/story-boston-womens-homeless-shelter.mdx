In the spirit of helping our fellow human beings and building bridges among communities, Zahra Hasanaat has initiated regular meal service events at Rosie's Place, a homeless shelter for women in Boston, Massachusetts. To date, we have funded 5 dinners and organized the volunteers for each event. We have partnered with a local Church, Masjid, and Synagogue, including student groups and have worked together with these communities to improve the lives of poor and homeless women.
