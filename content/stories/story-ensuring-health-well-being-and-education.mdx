Zafar is a glassworker whose hands were injured in an accident. Zahra Hasanaat has provided him with funds for his children’s education, and medical and relief aid during the pandemic.

**Socioeconomic Background**

Zafar lives in a house in the slums; he is illiterate. He used to work in a glass store, however, when his hands were injured in an accident with glass shards, it made it difficult for him to continue.

**Immediate Issue**

He has fees for his children’s school, along with medical issues that required financial assistance. During the pandemic, he also asked the organization for emergency help.

**Relief Aid Specifics**

QJSP has provided the funds for his children’s education, and Zahra Hasanaat has given him medical assistance and Covid relief aid during the pandemic.

**Benefits**

The immediate benefits are primarily associated with ensuring the health and well-being of Zafar and his family through medical and relief aid, along with the continued education of his children.
