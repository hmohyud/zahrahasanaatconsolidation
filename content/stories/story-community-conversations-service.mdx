Our exemplars have always stressed the importance and necessity of extending a helping hand to the underprivileged. We invite you to meet with our community’s social service champions – individuals who have made it their life mission to help others. During this live interactive session we heard them share their stories and what motivated them to spend their life helping others.

On November 5th, we met with our community’s social service champions – individuals who have made it their life mission to help others. We heard them share their stories and what motivated them to spend their life helping others.

Each of them comes from a different generation, a diverse background, and a different walk of life; yet they all share a common ethos – to fulfill their duty of helping those in need.
