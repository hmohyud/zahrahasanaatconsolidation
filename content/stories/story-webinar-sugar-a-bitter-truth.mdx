The Fatemi Dawat Mas’ul Coordination Committee with Zahra Hasanaat Health was pleased to announce a webinar on Diabetes and Obesity. This was a live interactive webinar session, the fifth in the Community Conversations Series, about two very serious diseases that impact a lot of people. Unfortunately there are 72.96 million cases of diabetes in the adult population of India today and it is said to affect one in every ten people worldwide. Obesity is a medical problem that increases the risk of other medical issues and health problems, such as heart disease and diabetes. In this webinar, our esteemed panelists of doctors and nutritionists provided valuable information and discuss and share advice for healthy living.
