In February 2023, Zahra Hasanaat ran a camp for eye check ups in partnership with Isha Netralaya (Isha Eye Care).

The workshop was free and open for all and services provided included eye screening and subsidized eyeglasses, providing medicines to address allergy and other eye conditions, and identification of cataract and other surgical cases.

For eye camp participants, specially subsidized rates were offered for prescription eyeglasses, as well as cataract surgery done with Isha Netralaya.

The Zahra Hasanaat Health Committee is continuing to develop other partnerships and initiatives also that will help people achieve better health and wellbeing.
