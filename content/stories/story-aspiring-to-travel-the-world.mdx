Zainab is a career-oriented student whose dream is to travel the world as an air hostess. QJSP has granted her a full scholarship for two years to pursue this goal through a commerce degree.

**Socioeconomic & Family Background**

Initially, Zainab’s school fees were supported by one of her relatives. When prompted by her grandmother who felt very connected to Syedna Saheb she sent an application to QJSP. The organization supported her for one year with a full scholarship after which the next two years she was able to secure other grants for her tuition.

She is pursuing her degree in commerce at a college in Pune. She aspires to have a job one day that will allow her to travel the world.
